## Design Context

### Users
Echoes is a Chinese-first personal brand blog for professional visitors. People arrive here to quickly understand the author's taste, depth, and craft through polished writing, thoughtful curation, and a calm but high-standard reading experience. The primary jobs are discovering articles, evaluating the author's point of view, and joining lightweight community discussion through reactions and comments.

### Brand Personality
Cold-luxury, editorial, and precise. The interface should feel calm, credible, and highly considered, with the emotional goal of making visitors trust the author's judgment and enjoy staying in the content.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial and paper-like, drawing from the calm precision of `innei.in / Yohaku` without copying it literally: graphite and ivory dual themes, restrained warm accents, strong typography, generous whitespace, sidebar-like notes, and quiet motion. Light, system, and dark themes should all feel intentionally designed. The site should explicitly avoid generic AI glassmorphism, neon gradients, overly playful motifs, and dashboard-style metric clutter.

### Design Principles
1. Content is the hero: every layout choice should make reading feel easier and more premium.
2. Precision over ornament: use few visual ideas, but execute them meticulously across mobile and desktop.
3. Motion must clarify state: subtle reveals, smooth transitions, and reduced-motion support by default.
4. Community should feel lightweight: interaction is welcome, but it must never overpower the writing.
5. Notes and margins matter: side information should feel like annotations around the essay, not competing cards.
6. Theme parity matters: light, system, and dark modes should each feel fully designed, not primary plus afterthought.
